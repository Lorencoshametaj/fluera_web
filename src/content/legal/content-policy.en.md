## 1. What this is

Fluera lets you publish handwritten and hand-drawn **study material** — notes,
worksheets, diagrams, solved problems, mind maps, revision sheets — so other
learners can study from them. This policy explains what you may and may not
publish. It applies to everything you upload: the ink and strokes, the title,
the description, the tags, and any preview image generated from your work.

By publishing a Seed you agree to follow this policy. Breaking it can cost you
your post, and repeated or serious breaches can cost you your account. This
policy works together with the Marketplace / Creator Terms.

## 2. What's allowed

- Original educational material you made yourself: class notes, summaries,
  worksheets, formula sheets, annotated diagrams, worked solutions, flashcard
  sets, revision guides.
- Content across any subject, in any language, as long as it follows the rules
  below.
- Study material you have the right to share (see the copyright rules in §3).

Keep it useful, keep it real. This is a place to help people learn.

## 3. What is prohibited

The following are **not allowed** anywhere on the marketplace. Publishing them
will get the content removed and may get your account suspended or banned.

- **Sexual content or nudity.** No pornography, sexually explicit drawings or
  writing, or sexual content of any kind. This is a marketplace for study
  material, not adult content.
  - **Anything sexual involving a minor is zero tolerance.** Child sexual abuse
    material (CSAM) is illegal. We remove it immediately, preserve the evidence,
    and **report it to the relevant authorities** (for example NCMEC and/or
    national law-enforcement and child-protection agencies). We may permanently
    ban the account without warning.
- **Graphic violence or gore.** No shock content, no glorification of violence,
  no gratuitous depictions of injury or death. (Legitimate medical or forensic
  study material is handled separately — see §4.)
- **Hate and harassment.** No content that attacks, dehumanises, or incites
  hatred or violence against people based on race, ethnicity, national origin,
  religion, disability, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age,
  or any other protected characteristic. No bullying or targeted harassment.
- **Illegal content or activity.** No content that is illegal, or that
  instructs, promotes, or facilitates serious harm (e.g. weapons, explosives,
  or drug manufacture, or other criminal activity).
- **Credible threats.** No threats of violence or harm against any person or
  group.
- **Other people's personal data (doxxing).** Do not publish private or
  identifying information about other people — names paired with addresses,
  phone numbers, ID numbers, school or workplace, private photos, etc. If real
  people appear in your notes, do not expose their personal data.
- **Spam and bulk low-effort content.** No spam, mass-duplicated uploads,
  scams, misleading titles, keyword stuffing, advertising, or off-topic
  filler dressed up as study material.
- **Copyright infringement.** Do not upload material you don't have the right to
  share — scanned textbooks, past exam papers, paid courses, or other people's
  work reproduced without permission. Rights holders can request removal (see
  the DMCA / copyright process). Publish your **own** notes.

## 4. The "content maturity" flag — what it is and is **not**

Some genuine study material is **sensitive or graphic for legitimate
educational reasons** — for example anatomy and dissection diagrams, clinical or
medical imagery, or forensic-science material. That content can be allowed, but
it should not be shown to everyone by default. For those cases you can mark a
Seed with the **content-maturity flag**, which gates it to an **18+** audience.

**The maturity flag is only for legitimately sensitive or graphic educational
content.** It is **not** a way to publish adult, sexual, or otherwise prohibited
material. Sexual content and nudity are prohibited outright (§3) and cannot be
made acceptable by flagging them as "mature." Mis-flagging prohibited content is
itself a violation and will be treated as an attempt to evade this policy.

**How gating works.** Maturity-flagged Seeds are **hidden by default** and are
shown only after you explicitly confirm you are **18 or older**; they are never
surfaced to browsing that has not affirmed 18+. Because the marketplace can host
such 18+-gated educational material, the app is age-rated accordingly on the app
stores.

## 5. How enforcement works

- **Pre-publication review.** Seeds are screened before they go live. Screening
  uses a combination of **automated classification and human moderation**.
- **Removal.** Content that breaks this policy is removed. Serious or illegal
  content (especially child-safety cases) is removed immediately and, where
  required, reported to authorities and preserved as evidence.
- **Strikes.** Violations are recorded against your account. Repeated
  violations escalate — a warning, then temporary restrictions, then
  suspension.
- **Bans.** Severe violations (such as CSAM, credible threats, or repeated
  serious breaches) can result in an immediate and permanent ban.
- **Appeals.** If you believe a decision was wrong, you can appeal. A human
  reviews appeals, and we will give you a reason for the decision. (Note:
  decisions on clearly illegal content, such as child-safety removals, may not
  be reversible.)

We may also remove content or restrict accounts where we are legally required
to, or to protect the safety of our users.

## 6. How to report content

**Anyone can report a Seed** — you don't need a Fluera account, and you don't
have to be the person affected. Use the report control on the content itself or
on its public share page, or email **lorenco@fluera.dev**. Reports go to the same
moderation queue that reviewers work from.

When you report, please choose the reason that fits best:

| Report reason  | Use it for |
|----------------|------------|
| `child-safety` | Any sexual content involving a minor, or content that endangers a child. **Highest priority.** |
| `sexual`       | Pornography, nudity, or other sexual content. |
| `violence`     | Graphic violence, gore, or credible threats of harm. |
| `hate`         | Hate speech, harassment, or attacks on protected groups. |
| `copyright`    | Content that infringes your (or someone else's) copyright. |
| `pii`          | Someone's private or identifying personal data (doxxing). |
| `spam`         | Spam, scams, bulk duplicates, or misleading / off-topic content. |
| `other`        | Anything else that breaks this policy. |

Please report in good faith. Deliberately false or abusive reports may
themselves lead to action against your account.

## 7. Block a creator

Separately from reporting, in the app you can **block a creator** (identified by
their pseudonymous author code). Once blocked, that creator's Seeds stop
appearing for you across the marketplace — in browse, search, featured, and on
share pages — and you can unblock them at any time. Blocking is **personal to
you** (it changes what *you* see); reporting is what asks *us* to review content.
Filtering (pre-publication review), reporting, blocking, and a published contact
(`lorenco@fluera.dev`) together give every reader control over what they see.
